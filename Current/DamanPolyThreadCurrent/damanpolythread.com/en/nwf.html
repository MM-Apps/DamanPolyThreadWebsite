Narrow Woven Fabrics

High in strength, longevity, and durability


TUFF' and 'DPL' are the brand names for Webbing or Narrow Woven Fabrics manufactured by Daman Polythread LTD. High in strength, longevity, and durability; these fabrics are manufactured from virgin material. Available in different yarns line PP yarn , Polyester yarn , Nylon yarn along with various patterns and designs with a width range of 10 mm to 100 mm.

We also offer custom designing solutions to our clients. Our clients have the liberty to customize their own designs as per the requirement. These things have contributed in making Daman Polythread one of the most respected Webbing Manufactures in India.
